# Mezcal Diaries: what Claude built (draft for Siona's review)

Written 31 Oct 2026. This is a **Claude generated draft** for Siona to review. Nothing here is public: every article is hidden, and nothing needs your sign-off to stay that way. Working title only: "Mezcal Diaries" can be renamed.

## Why this exists

Mezcalómano sells a 40 agave card deck, and most people searching for mezcal do not know our name. A section of short, honest articles answers the questions those people already type into Google (what is mezcal, is it smoky, mezcal vs tequila, what to buy a mezcal lover). Each article answers one question in its first paragraph, uses our deck data (the flavor compass, the 40 agaves) as the proof, and points to the deck and the directory. The plan came from the SEO article plan (Claude Doc: https://claude.ai/artifact/WrtLaXaBLdTwBoxEAc6RWM), which said /journal. Kimber chose **/diaries**.

## What was built on the site

- A new section at mezcalomano.com/diaries (list page) and mezcalomano.com/diaries/<article-name> (one page per article), in the site design.
- 18 draft articles, all written by Claude from our own data (Species_Final, the Cards CSV, the directory) plus public sources named in each article's review notes.
- A **draft switch**. A draft opens only at its direct link, shows a yellow banner at the top listing what must be checked, is marked "noindex" (Google is told to ignore it), has no structured data, and does not appear in the list, the menu or the sitemap. Publishing one is a one line change by Kimber; then it appears in the list, the sitemap and a "MEZCAL DIARIES" menu item.
- Important: drafts are **not password protected**. Anyone who guesses a link could open one. The links are not linked from anywhere. A private preview link is possible if we want it.

## Rules the drafts follow

1. **Agave first.** No brands, producers, shops or products in the body (Kimber, 31 Oct). Agave names, COMERCAM and our own Discovery Deck (labeled as ours) are fine. Source names may appear in the review notes only.
2. American English, no em dashes or en dashes.
3. Every article opens with a plain answer, then headed sections, a short FAQ, and links to related directory pages.
4. Siona has final sign-off on customer facing copy.

## What Claude checked (31 Oct 2026)

- The legal wording on artesanal, ancestral, the mezcal categories, aging terms and what a label must show was checked against the **official English translation of NOM-070-SCFI-2016** hosted by COMERCAM. Several errors were fixed (how ancestral mezcal is cooked and distilled, the 35 to 55 percent strength range, "lot number and numbered certification seal" instead of a "producer code", the aging terms, and a wrong claim about additives). The Spanish text prevails in any dispute, so someone who reads Spanish should confirm.
- Tequila wording now says "100% agave tequila" because mixto tequila can use other sugars. The tequila statements have not been checked against the tequila standard.
- Our counts were rechecked against the live Species_Final sheet: 15 wild or semi wild agaves, 6 cultivated, and for smoke 21 clean, 17 toasty, 2 smoky. The espadín share is 84.53 percent of certified production in 2025 (COMERCAM Informe Estadístico 2026, p. 7, Tabla 2).
- Data slips found in the card data: Churique's compass and Bicuixe's nickname are already fixed in the live sheet. The face card spelling "Jimedor" and the line "Yeilds ripe piñas" still need checking against the printed deck.
